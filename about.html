My Story

From Exhausted Mom to Wellness Coach

Hi, I'm Sarah Mitchell. Five years ago, I was a 34-year-old mom of two who couldn't recognize herself in the mirror. I was tired, overweight, and had lost all confidence in my body.

Today, I've transformed not just my body—but my entire relationship with fitness, food, and self-worth. And I've made it my mission to help other women do the same.

My Fitness Journey

After my second child, I hit rock bottom. I tried every diet under the sun—keto, intermittent fasting, juice cleanses. I'd lose 10 pounds, then gain 15 back. The cycle was exhausting and demoralizing.

The turning point came when I stopped trying to punish my body into change and started listening to it instead. I swapped extreme diets for balanced nutrition. I traded grueling gym sessions for 10-minute home workouts I could actually stick to.

"I didn't lose weight by hating my body. I transformed by finally loving it enough to take care of it."

Over 18 months, I lost 25 pounds—not through deprivation, but through sustainable habits. More importantly, I gained energy, confidence, and a deep understanding of what women's bodies actually need.

That experience led me to get certified as a wellness coach, and BloomFit was born—a space where women can find real, compassionate guidance on their wellness journeys.
